Screenshot2Meet Privacy Policy

Effective October 17, 2026

In short
  • The calendar screenshots you add are sent over HTTPS to the Screenshot2Meet service, read in memory, and then discarded. They are never saved or shared.
  • There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and your data is never sold.
  • Your settings stay in your own browser.

What Screenshot2Meet does

Screenshot2Meet is a Chrome extension that fills in your availability on a When2meet event from screenshots of your calendar. You add screenshots in the extension's side panel. The Screenshot2Meet service reads the events in them and works out which time slots you are free. The extension then marks those slots on the When2meet page you have open, the same way you would by clicking.

Information sent to the service

When you click Fill When2meet, the extension sends the service only what it needs for that fill:

WhatWhy
The screenshots you add To find the events and their times. Anything visible in a screenshot, such as event titles, is part of what is read.
Details of the When2meet event: its time slots, its dates or days of the week, and its time zone To match your calendar events to the event's slots.
The events read from your screenshots (day, start and end time, title, colour) Sent back to the service to work out which slots are free.
Your Screenshot2Meet settings (OCR languages, keywords and colours to ignore, buffer time, and similar) To apply your preferences when reading screenshots and choosing slots.

The extension does not send your When2meet name, your When2meet password, other people's availability, your browsing history, or anything from other websites.

How the service handles your information

  • All requests go over an encrypted HTTPS connection.
  • Screenshots and event details are processed in memory only to answer your request, then discarded. They are not written to disk, kept in a database, or logged.
  • Your information is not shared with or sold to anyone, and is not used to train models, for advertising, or for anything other than filling in your When2meet availability.

Hosting and technical logs

The service runs on Fly.io in the United States. Like most web services, the hosting platform keeps short-term technical logs of requests: the time, your IP address, the address requested (for example /extract), and whether it succeeded. These logs do not contain your screenshots or event details. They are used only to keep the service running and to investigate problems, and are kept according to Fly.io's log retention.

To prevent abuse, the service also counts how many requests each IP address makes. These counts are kept in memory for up to one hour and then discarded.

Information on the When2meet page

When you use the side panel, the extension reads the When2meet event open in your current tab: its time slots, time zone, whether you are signed in, and your current availability. It uses this to fill in, and if you ask, undo, your own availability. It only changes your availability. It does not change other people's responses or the event itself.

Information stored in your browser

  • Settings are saved in your browser's extension storage, so they are still there next time. They stay until you change them or remove the extension.
  • Undo history (your previous availability on an event, so you can undo a fill) is kept in session storage and is cleared when you close the browser.

This stored information never leaves your computer, except for the settings listed in the table above, which are sent with each fill.

Your choices

  • Only the screenshots you choose to add are ever sent. You can crop out anything you'd rather not share before adding a screenshot.
  • Removing the extension deletes its settings and undo history from your browser.

Chrome Web Store user data policy

Screenshot2Meet's use of information received through the extension follows the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Children

Screenshot2Meet is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the extension's release notes.
